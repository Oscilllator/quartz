I noticed that the laser (one of the cheapest available from aliexpres) was not particularly stable. The fringes sometimes went away entirely, and the diffraction pattern drifted over a period of seconds. Adjusting the power supply you could get the laser back into a good operating regime which is not surprising. I wondered if the temperature of the laser was important so gave it a hit of the ol freezer spray, and noticed that the air current coming out of it was clearly visible. 

Turns out the [mach diamonds](https://youtu.be/DfYlLns0els?t=579) that come out the end of the compressed air is also visible.

![[Pasted image 20260418213657.png]]

So that's interesting. I was not able to see air currents from a blowtorch or a hot soldering iron though.
