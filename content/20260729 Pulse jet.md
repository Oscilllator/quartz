Recently boom supersonic, the supersonic jet airliner startup, [released a challenge](https://boomsupersonic.com/prize). Make a supersonic RC plane, and get $800k. There is no chance whatsoever that I'll be able to do this by manufacturing my own jet engine, and trying to win the competition with the RC jet engines designed for operation around mach ~0.7 seems an expensive and dubious proposition.

There is such a thing as a jet engine where the compressor is powered by an electric motor, [here's a video of one](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3SVBqCBzmpU). However everything I've seen suggests that the compressor power required is just implausibly high, a minimum of 10's of kW for a pulse jet with a 150mm diameter.

But. Pulse jets operate well at low speeds but not at high speeds. Ram jets operate well at high speeds but not at low speeds. Both devices are a tube with no moving parts. Could they be combined to get a device that can go from 0 -> Mach 1?.
